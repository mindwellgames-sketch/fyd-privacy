Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2025

Our Commitment: Feed Your Dragons does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Your privacy is absolute.

Information We Do NOT Collect

Feed Your Dragons is designed with privacy-first principles. We do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Photos, camera, or microphone access
  • Any personal information whatsoever

How Your Data is Stored

All app data is stored locally on your device only using iOS's secure UserDefaults storage:

  • Selected age group
  • Game progress and streaks
  • Unlocked badges and achievements
  • Audio preferences
  • Parental consent timestamp (for ages 10-14)

This data never leaves your device and is completely private to you.

No Third-Party Services

Feed Your Dragons does not use:

  • Analytics services (no Firebase, Google Analytics, etc.)
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media integrations
  • Cloud storage or backup services
  • Any third-party data collection tools

No Internet Required

The app works completely offline. No internet connection is required or used for gameplay, which means:

  • No data transmission to external servers
  • No tracking of app usage
  • Complete privacy and security

COPPA Compliance

Feed Your Dragons is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • No personal information collected from children
  • Parental gate for ages 10-14
  • Age-appropriate content only
  • No advertising or in-app purchases
  • No account creation required

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Protected by your device's built-in security (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID)
  • Backed up only if you use iCloud or iTunes backup
  • Deleted completely when you uninstall the app

Parental Controls

Parents and guardians can:

  • Reset all app data at any time via Settings
  • Monitor app usage through iOS Screen Time
  • Delete the app and all associated data instantly

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect app improvements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Feed Your Dragons work?

Feed Your Dragons is a scenario-based wellness game that helps kids and teens (ages 10-17) build resilience through real-life choices. Navigate 900+ age-appropriate scenarios and watch your wise dragon grow!

Is my data safe?

Yes! All your progress is stored locally on your device only. We don't collect, store, or share any personal information. Your privacy is our priority.

What age groups is this for?

Feed Your Dragons is designed for ages 10-17, with content specifically tailored for three age groups: 10-12, 13-14, and 15-17.

Does it require internet?

No! Feed Your Dragons works completely offline. No internet connection required.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings (gear icon) and select "Reset All Data" to start fresh.

What's the difference between Wellness Game Mode and Reality Check Mode?

Wellness Game Mode: Learn and track your progress, earn badges and achievements.
Reality Check Mode: Practice with honest self-assessment without any tracking or pressure.
