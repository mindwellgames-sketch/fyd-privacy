# Privacy Policy for Feed Your Dragons

**Last Updated:** [Current Date]

## Introduction

Mindwell Games ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the FYD2 mobile application (the "App").

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.**

The Feed Your Dragons app is designed with privacy in mind:
- No account creation or registration required
- No personal information requested
- No data transmitted to external servers
- No analytics or tracking
- No advertising or third-party data sharing

## Local Data Storage

The App stores the following information **locally on your device only**:

### Game Progress Data
- Your current streak count
- Highest streak achieved
- Badge unlock progress
- Category completion status
- Age group selection

### App Preferences
- Music on/off preference
- Selected age group

**Important:** This data is stored only on your device using iOS UserDefaults. We cannot access this data, and it is not transmitted anywhere outside your device.

## Data You Provide

The App processes scenario content that you interact with, but this interaction data:
- Remains on your device
- Is never transmitted to us or any third party
- Is used only to track your game progress locally

## Third-Party Services

The App uses the Lottie animation library (Lottie-iOS) to display dragon animations. Lottie does not collect or transmit any user data. You can review Lottie's privacy practices at: https://github.com/airbnb/lottie-ios

## Children's Privacy

The Feed Your Dragons app is designed for users ages 10-17. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

- We do not collect any personal information from children
- We do not allow children to make their information publicly available
- We do not enable children to communicate with other users
- All data remains local to the device

**For Parents/Guardians:** Because we do not collect any personal information, there is no data for you to review or delete. Game progress can be reset at any time within the app settings.

## Educational Nature of Content

Feed Your Dragons is an educational app that presents scenario-based content to help teens build resilience and wellness skills. The content is:
- Based on child development research and emotional wellness principles
- Educational in nature, not therapeutic
- Age-appropriate for teens ages 10-17
- Reviewed for safety and appropriateness

**Important Disclaimer:** This app is not a substitute for professional mental health care. If you or someone you know is experiencing a mental health crisis, please contact a trusted adult or call Kids Help Phone at 1-800-668-6868.

## Data Security

Since all data remains on your device:
- Your data is protected by your device's security features (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID)
- Data is backed up with your device backups (if enabled in iOS Settings)
- Deleting the app will delete all locally stored progress

## Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:
- **Access:** All your data is visible within the app (Trophy Case, Settings)
- **Delete:** You can reset your progress at any time in Settings, or delete the app entirely
- **Export:** Currently not supported (all data is local only)

## Changes to Stored Data

If you delete the app, all locally stored data (streaks, badges, preferences) will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

## International Users

The App is available globally. All data processing (which is minimal) occurs locally on your device, regardless of your location.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Providing an in-app notification (for material changes)

Your continued use of the App after changes indicates acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
